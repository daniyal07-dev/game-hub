🧠 How does the AI Autopilot work?

The AI uses Breadth-First Search (BFS), a pathfinding algorithm. Every move it looks at the grid like a map, spreads outwards from the snake's head one step at a time and finds the shortest safe path to the food. Before it commits, it checks that after eating there is still a way to reach its own tail, so it never traps itself. If there is no safe path to the food, it follows its tail or picks the move with the most open space.
