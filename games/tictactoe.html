🧠 How does the unbeatable AI work?

The AI uses an algorithm called Minimax. Before it moves, it imagines every possible future of the game: its move, your reply, its next move, and so on until the game ends. It scores each ending (win, draw, loss) and picks the move that leads to the best result even if you play perfectly. It also uses alpha-beta pruning to skip branches that cannot matter, so it is fast. Because Tic Tac Toe is small, the AI can see everything, so the best you can get is a draw.
